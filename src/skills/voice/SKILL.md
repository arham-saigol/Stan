# Voice

1. Read `voice_profile` and a bounded selection from `voice_examples`. Read `strategy` for the current audience and purpose; read `playbook` only when analytics or a proven lesson matters.
2. Establish the post's one useful idea, intended reader, and conversational context. Research claims that need current evidence.
3. Write from the idea rather than a reusable social template. Prefer concrete language, natural rhythm, and the shortest complete version.
4. Compare the draft with the loaded positive and negative examples. Remove generic hooks, fake urgency, unsupported certainty, and phrases that could belong to anyone.
5. Return the requested alternatives or final draft. Keep drafts private unless the current owner turn separately authorizes the matching X operation.

Complete when every factual claim is supportable, the draft fits its X format, and its wording reflects the loaded voice evidence rather than invented preferences.
